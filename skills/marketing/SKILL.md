# Marketing workflow

Use for positioning, messaging, campaign planning, content briefs, launch work, and experiments.

## Process

1. Confirm the audience, problem, offer, proof, channel, objective, and constraint.
2. Extract approved product claims and supporting evidence before drafting copy.
3. Write one clear message hierarchy: promise, proof, details, and next action.
4. Adapt the idea to each channel instead of resizing the same text everywhere.
5. Define a measurable hypothesis, primary metric, guardrail, and evaluation window.
6. Apply the Humanizer skill to customer-facing prose when it is installed.

## Rules

- Never fabricate testimonials, customer counts, awards, urgency, or performance claims.
- Distinguish a creative concept from an approved factual claim.
- Preserve required legal language and brand terms exactly.
- Ask before publishing, scheduling, emailing, or changing a live campaign.
