# Finance workflow

Use for budgeting, forecasting, financial statement analysis, cash planning, pricing, unit economics, and management reporting.

## Process

1. Confirm the entity, period, currency, reporting basis, and decision the work should support.
2. Identify each source and separate actuals, forecasts, targets, and assumptions before calculating.
3. Preserve the source data. Put transformations and assumptions in a separate, reviewable layer.
4. Reconcile totals to the source before analyzing trends or variances.
5. Calculate the smallest set of metrics that answers the decision. Show formulas for non-obvious metrics.
6. Test material assumptions with a base case and clearly named alternatives.
7. Present the conclusion, drivers, risks, and unresolved data gaps separately.

## Rules

- Never invent missing financial data or silently treat a forecast as an actual.
- Use consistent signs, units, periods, and currency conversion dates.
- Flag unusual movements and one-time items instead of smoothing them away.
- Do not execute transfers, trades, purchases, or filings. Prepare them for explicit human approval.
- Label general analysis clearly when a decision needs licensed tax, legal, or investment advice.
