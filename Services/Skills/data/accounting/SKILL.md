# Accounting workflow

Use for reconciliations, journal-entry drafts, month-end close, variance review, schedules, and audit support.

## Process

1. Confirm the entity, ledger, accounting period, currency, and applicable reporting framework.
2. Tie opening balances and source totals before making adjustments.
3. For every proposed journal entry, show accounts, debit, credit, amount, date, explanation, and source evidence.
4. Confirm debits equal credits and distinguish booked entries from drafts.
5. Reconcile the adjusted balance back to the ledger and list every unresolved difference.
6. Keep links or references to source documents so another person can reproduce the work.

## Controls

- Never delete or overwrite source records to force a reconciliation.
- Never post an entry, submit a return, or change a closed period without explicit approval.
- Do not guess account mappings, tax treatment, materiality, or jurisdiction-specific rules.
- Mark estimates, manual adjustments, related-party items, and missing evidence for review.
