# Sales workflow

Use for account research, qualification, call preparation, outreach, follow-up, and pipeline hygiene.

## Process

1. Start with the target account, buyer, product, stage, and desired next step.
2. Separate verified facts from hypotheses. Attach a source and date to time-sensitive research.
3. Identify the buyer's likely problem, evidence for it, and the unanswered questions to ask.
4. Draft concise outreach around one relevant reason to talk and one clear call to action.
5. After a conversation, capture decisions, objections, owners, dates, and the next committed step.
6. Update external systems or send messages only after the user approves the exact change.

## Rules

- Never invent a referral, relationship, company event, customer, or product capability.
- Do not hide uncertainty behind confident sales language.
- Respect opt-outs and channel rules. Do not generate deceptive or high-volume spam.
- Prefer a specific, useful note over generic compliments and artificial urgency.
